Chief Complaint:
48-year-old patient presents with 10 weeks of severe lower-back pain radiating down the posterior left leg to the great toe.

Physical Examination:
Lumbar flexion limited by pain. Positive left straight-leg raise at 35 degrees. Decreased light-touch sensation along the left L5 dermatome and 4/5 weakness in left extensor hallucis longus. Patellar and Achilles reflexes 2+ bilaterally.

Prior Diagnostic Testing:
Lumbar spine X-ray performed 6 weeks ago demonstrated mild disc space narrowing at L4-L5 without spondylolisthesis or acute fracture.

Conservative Treatment History:
Patient completed 6 weeks of supervised physical therapy (twice weekly) and took naproxen 500 mg BID plus gabapentin 300 mg TID for 6 weeks with no improvement in radicular symptoms.

Assessment and Plan:
Persistent left L5 radiculopathy refractory to 6 weeks of conservative management. Requesting MRI of the lumbar spine without contrast to evaluate for L4-L5 disc herniation and nerve root compression prior to considering epidural steroid injection.
